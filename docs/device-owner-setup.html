Device Owner Setup

Phone setup steps

The APK is one app. Android decides when that app becomes Device Owner during managed-device provisioning.

1. Prepare the phone
Back up anything important, then use a fresh/unprovisioned phone for the Device Owner setup.
2. Download the APK
Download App + Device Owner APK. It is the same Nadeem Mobiles app.
3. Complete Android managed-device provisioning
A normal APK install is not enough to become Device Owner. Use a supported Android provisioning method for your phone/Android version (for example, zero-touch, NFC, or a controlled setup/ADB method).
4. Pair the customer
After Device Owner setup finishes, open Nadeem Mobiles and enter the 6-digit code generated for that customer in the dashboard. No server address and no QR code are used by the app.
5. During enrollment
After successful pairing, the app keeps the phone under Nadeem Mobiles management. The current policy blocks normal factory reset and uninstall while the device remains enrolled.
6. When the phone is released
The shop sends Release from the dashboard. The app clears its management state, acknowledges the release to the backend, and opens the Android uninstall flow.
