Download Nadeem Mobiles App

This is the same APK used by the Nadeem Mobiles phone app and for Android Device Owner / managed-device provisioning.

Download App + Device Owner APK
The phone app uses only the 6-digit pairing code. There is no server-address field and no QR-code screen in the app.
Important: downloading or opening an APK does not, by itself, make it Device Owner. Android must complete the managed-device provisioning step on a fresh/unprovisioned phone.
After Device Owner provisioning is complete, open the Nadeem Mobiles app and enter the 6-digit code from the dashboard. Once pairing succeeds, the app applies the enrollment protections and stays managed until the shop sends Release.
